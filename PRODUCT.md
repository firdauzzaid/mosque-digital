# Mosque Display

An offline-first information display for a mosque TV or Android STB. The
operator configures it on the device and visitors see prayer times,
announcements, media, agenda, Friday officers, and finance information.

The display follows the device's local time. At each eligible prayer it plays
tarhim then murottal for 15 minutes before adhan, plays adhan, shows iqamah
countdown, and becomes black for 15 minutes after iqamah. The black screen is
an application screen, not a command to turn off the TV.
